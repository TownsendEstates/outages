Your AI Line: how it works

For Aaron Robinson, REALTOR®, Century 21 Masters · Radical Robinson

Set up and supported by Scott Townsend · 587.200.4953

The short version

Your cell number does not change. Your phone rings first, every time. If you miss a call, decline it, or have no signal, it goes to your AI assistant instead of voicemail. The assistant tells callers it is an AI, names Century 21 Masters, takes their details, books time on your calendar, and texts and emails you a summary within a minute or two.

What the assistant does

  • Gets name, number, email, buying or selling, area or address, timeline, preapproval (yes or no only), and best time to call.
  • Books callbacks, buyer and listing consultations, and showing requests on your calendar.
  • Creates new leads in Follow Up Boss (source "Radical Robinson AI Line") or adds a note to existing contacts, assigned to you.
  • Urgent calls (offer or condition deadline today, closing problems): texts you instantly and tries to put the caller through to you live.

What it will never do

  • Give opinions on price, value, offers, contracts, legal, or mortgages. It says you will answer personally.
  • Promise results, mention incentives, or say a home is available or sold.
  • Text or email your callers, or sign anyone up for anything.

Your alerts

After every call you get a text and an email: caller, number, reason, summary, next step and a link to the transcript. URGENT or VIP calls are flagged in the first line.

Your VIP list

VIPs (family, business partners) are greeted by name if they reach the assistant, and you get an instant text. Family stays out of Follow Up Boss.

  1. Open <your AI Line link>/admin on your phone and enter your PIN.
  2. Tap VIP list > Add VIP. Enter the name the assistant should use, the number and the relationship. Save.
  3. Choose Critical if you want the assistant to try to put them through live.
  4. Also add them to Favourites (iPhone) or Starred (Android) so they ring through Do Not Disturb and Focus.

To change or remove someone, tap Edit. Changes work on the next call.

Compliance notes

  • Every call opens with an AI disclosure and a recording notice. Callers can say no to recording.
  • Century 21 Masters is named on every call. Please get your broker's OK before we go live.
  • You stay accountable for what is said for you. Skim the transcripts in the first two weeks.
  • If someone asks you to delete their information: admin page > Privacy request, then delete them in Follow Up Boss. Reply within 30 days.
  • Recordings are removed after 90 days and transcripts after 2 years.
  • Please do not turn on automatic drip texts or emails for AI Line leads in Follow Up Boss until we have checked them against anti-spam rules (CASL).
